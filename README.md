# Psychological Thriller

An exploratory long-form psychological thriller — a work in progress.

## About the Project

This is my first long-form fiction project.

I am writing it without a fixed ending or a complete plot outline. I don't yet know exactly where the story will go, how it will develop, or what its final form will be. Some of the story will be discovered through the writing itself.

The story explores dreams, memory, perception, and the boundary between what we experience and what we understand.

## Feedback

This project is being shared publicly because I want to learn from readers and improve my writing.

Constructive feedback on the sections that have already been written is welcome, particularly on:

* Writing and clarity
* Dialogue
* Pacing
* Characterization
* Reader experience

I may revise sections based on suggestions when I feel they fit the story.

However, I won't be sharing unreleased plot ideas, future developments, twists, or private notes about the story.
